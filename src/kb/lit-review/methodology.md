# Literature Synthesis Guide

## Purpose

Use this guide to move from annotated bibliography to real synthesis.

## Synthesis Moves

- Group studies by theme, method, theory, period, case, or debate.
- Compare what different studies explain well and where they are limited.
- Identify relationships among concepts, not just author-by-author claims.
- Separate empirical findings from theoretical arguments.
- Show how the user's topic emerges from the literature.

## Structure Options

### Theme-Based

Best when the literature clusters around several substantive issues.

### Debate-Based

Best when the literature contains competing explanations or interpretations.

### Method-Based

Best when methods shape findings or when the user asks for methodology review.

### Chronological

Use only when temporal development is analytically meaningful.

## Anti-Pattern

Do not write one paragraph per paper unless the user explicitly asks for an annotated bibliography.
