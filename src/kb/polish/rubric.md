# Polish and Transcreation Rubric

## Core Goal

The polish/transcreation tool should improve academic expression while preserving the user's claims, evidence, structure, and intended discipline.

## Chinese Polish Checks

- Does not change claims, data, citations, or structure unless asked.
- Removes cliches, exaggeration, and empty significance claims.
- Improves paragraph transitions and sentence rhythm.
- Keeps terminology consistent.
- Avoids sounding like policy slogans unless the selected genre requires it.

## English Transcreation Checks

- Re-expresses arguments in natural English academic prose.
- Avoids sentence-by-sentence translationese.
- Preserves technical content, citations, proper nouns, and data.
- Uses appropriate hedging.
- Follows discipline conventions for sociology, economics, psychology, education, management, political science, or general social science.

## Common Failures

- Adding claims that were not in the original.
- Over-polishing into vague formal language.
- Translating Chinese paragraph logic too literally into English.
- Repeating "This paper..." or "In recent years..." mechanically.
- Ignoring user-provided term mappings.
